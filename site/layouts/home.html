Deliberate Practice for Technical Leaders

Short exercises for everyday technical leadership

A kata is a small prepared response to a moment that keeps showing up.

The trigger arrives, you give it 10 to 15 minutes, you write something down, and you carry on with the day.

No workshop, no coach, no permission needed.

How to Use Them

Choose a Kata Pick one that feels relevant right now.

Practice It Work through the steps, alone or with your team.

Reflect and Repeat Note what you did, what changed, and what you'll try next.
